隐私政策

最后更新：2026-07-25

魔方岛为完全离线应用：不注册账号、不联网、不收集任何个人信息。

相机与图像

拍照识别贴纸颜色的全部计算在设备本机完成；图像仅用于当次识别，不保存、不上传、不用于任何其他用途。

本地数据

学习进度、设置偏好等数据只保存在设备本地存储中，不会同步到任何服务器；卸载应用即随之清除。

诊断日志

应用发生错误时，会在设备本地记录简短的运行时诊断信息（错误类型与堆栈，不含学习数据或图像），最多保留 200 条，超出自动覆盖最早的记录。这些记录只保存在本机，不会自动发送到任何地方；只有你在「我的 → 帮助与反馈」主动点击「导出诊断日志」时才会被复制出去（用于附在你发给我们的反馈里）。

网络与第三方

应用不发起任何网络请求，不集成第三方统计或广告 SDK，不与任何第三方共享数据。

青少年使用

应用不收集任何用户信息（含儿童用户），适合各年龄段学习者使用。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，或想反馈问题，请发信到 fanxtzen@gmail.com。

Cube Quest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

Cube Quest works fully offline: no account, no network access, no personal data collected.

Camera & Images

Sticker color recognition runs entirely on-device. Photos are used only for that single recognition and are never saved, uploaded, or used for any other purpose.

Local Data

Learning progress and settings are stored only in local device storage and never synced to any server; uninstalling the app removes them.

Diagnostics Log

When the app hits an error, it records a brief on-device runtime diagnostic entry (error type and stack trace only — no learning data or images), capped at 200 entries with the oldest automatically dropped. These entries stay on your device and are never sent anywhere automatically; they only leave the device if you tap "Export diagnostics log" under Profile → Help & Feedback (to attach to your feedback to us).

Network & Third Parties

The app makes no network requests and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs; no data is shared with any third party.

Use by Minors

The app collects no user information from anyone, including children, and is suitable for learners of all ages.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to send feedback, email fanxtzen@gmail.com.
