魔方岛 Cube Quest

技术支持

跟着关卡学魔方。遇到问题，我们来帮你。

联系我们

请发邮件至 fanxtzen@gmail.com。请注明设备型号、系统版本、应用版本，以及出现问题前的操作步骤。

相机不能使用

请在系统设置中检查魔方岛的相机权限。也可以使用手动涂色录入魔方。拍摄时请保持光线均匀，避免强烈反光，并检查识别后的颜色。

反馈与诊断日志

打开「我的 → 帮助与反馈」，点击「导出诊断日志」，按应用提示复制内容，再自行附在反馈邮件中。日志不会自动发送。发送前请检查内容，仅提供排查问题所需的信息。

进度与设置

学习进度和设置保存在当前设备中。卸载前请留意，本地进度可能随卸载清除。

隐私说明

应用的相机识别在设备本地进行。详情请阅读隐私政策。

Cube Quest

Support

Learn the cube, one lesson at a time. Need help? Get in touch.

Contact us

Email fanxtzen@gmail.com with your device model, operating system version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Camera troubleshooting

Check camera permission for Cube Quest in your device settings. You can also enter cube colors manually. Use even lighting, avoid glare, and review the recognized colors.

Feedback and diagnostics

Open Profile → Help & Feedback and select Export diagnostics log. Follow the app instructions to copy it, then attach it to your feedback email yourself. Logs are never sent automatically. Review the content before sending and include only information needed to investigate the issue.

Progress and settings

Your progress and settings are stored on the current device. Uninstalling the app may remove local progress.

Privacy

Camera recognition runs on your device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
